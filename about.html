About the platform

Connecting people with local professionals

Local Service Connector is a platform concept designed to make it easier for people to discover and connect with trusted local service providers.

The idea

One place to find help you can trust

Finding a reliable plumber, tutor, mechanic, or designer usually means asking around, scrolling through groups, or calling numbers that no longer work. Local Service Connector brings those professionals into one searchable catalogue with clear pricing, verified reviews, and real availability.

Customers can compare options side by side and send a structured service request in under a minute, while providers get a simple dashboard to receive, accept, and complete jobs.

Our mission

Make local services transparent, discoverable, and easy to book for everyone — regardless of technical confidence.

Our promise

Honest profiles, clear starting prices, and reviews from customers who actually completed a job.

How the platform works

  1. 1

    Search — pick a category, type what you need, and choose your city.

  2. 2

    Compare — review ratings, price ranges, availability, and working hours.

  3. 3

    Connect — send a service request and follow its status from your dashboard.

Demo disclaimer

Local Service Connector is a frontend prototype. Every provider, review, price, and statistic shown on this site is sample data held in the browser. Accounts, service requests, and saved providers are simulated with JavaScript and localStorage — nothing is sent to a server, and no personal information leaves your device.

A production version would add a real backend, database, payment layer, and identity provider. See the project README for the planned roadmap.
